Phases

The 3-phase plan

Skinny-fat at 58 kg means the goal is recomposition with a slight lean bulk — lose the belly softness while adding real muscle. No aggressive cuts. No dirty bulking.
Phase 1
Foundation & Reset
Days 1–30
Fix your gut first. Build the habit. Establish the baseline. You will not see dramatic visual change — but you'll feel completely different by day 30. This phase decides everything.
  • Heal digestion — anti-inflammatory, GERD-safe eating protocol
  • Establish 4-day gym split with progressive overload tracking
  • Hit 116g protein daily using eggs + plant protein
  • Introduce AM skincare routine (3 steps only)
  • Cut pre-workout to 3x/week to reduce acidity load
  • Nail sleep + wake time consistency (8am wake, 11pm sleep)
Phase 2
Build & Burn
Days 31–60
This is where the visual shift begins. Muscle is starting to show, belly fat is slowly reducing, skin is clearing up. Intensity goes up — rest periods tighten, volume increases.
  • Increase training volume by 10–15% (add sets, not exercises)
  • Add 1 day of zone-2 cardio (30 min brisk walk/cycling)
  • Introduce creatine monohydrate (5g/day)
  • Add PM skincare routine — actives begin (niacinamide)
  • Track waist measurement weekly (primary fat-loss metric)
  • Adjust calories if weight stalls beyond 3 weeks
Phase 3
Refine & Peak
Days 61–100
Push. This is where you lock in the results. Strength PRs, visible muscle definition, dramatically better skin, and a sustainable lifestyle you'll keep after day 100.
  • Introduce compound strength focus — aim for PR on bench, squat
  • Add second zone-2 cardio session (2x/week now)
  • Introduce AHA exfoliant in skincare 2x/week
  • Take progress photos vs. Day 1 — visual comparison
  • Fine-tune diet based on 60-day data (weight, energy, digestion)
  • Build the next 100-day plan from a much stronger baseline
GERD protocol — non-negotiable

No food within 2.5 hours of bed. No citrus, spicy food, or carbonated drinks. Pre-workout only with food — never empty stomach. Small, frequent meals over large ones. Eat sitting upright; don't lie down for 30–45 min post-meal.

Skincare protocol

Oily skin · Acne-prone · Dull · Dark spots · Large pores · Starting from zero. Simple, affordable, effective.
Golden rule for oily + acne skin

Don't skip moisturiser thinking it makes oiliness worse. Skipping it dehydrates skin → skin overproduces more oil → more breakouts. Lightweight gel moisturisers = your best friend.

AM routine — 5 minutes (Days 1–30)
1
Gentle face wash
Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser or Minimalist 2% Salicylic cleanser. Once in AM. Twice daily if very oily.
2
Niacinamide 10% serum
The Ordinary or Minimalist. Controls oil, reduces pores, fades dark spots. Apply 2–3 drops, wait 2 min.
3
SPF 50 sunscreen (non-negotiable)
Re'equil Ultramatte or Minimalist SPF 50. Apply every single morning. No sunscreen = no skin improvement.
PM routine — 5 minutes (Days 1–30)
1
Face wash (same cleanser)
Remove sweat, oil, SPF from the day. Don't use hot water — lukewarm only.
2
Niacinamide 10% serum
Same as AM — double application is fine and effective for oily/acne-prone skin.
3
Light gel moisturiser
Neutrogena Hydro Boost (water gel) or Minimalist Marula oil-free moisturiser. Small pea-sized amount.
Add from Day 31 — AHA exfoliant
+
Glycolic acid 7% toner or AHA 30%+BHA 2% mask
The Ordinary AHA 30%+BHA 2% peeling solution — use ONLY 2×/week (Thursday + Sunday PM). Replaces niacinamide on those nights. Addresses dullness, dark spots, texture dramatically. Do NOT use daily.
Add from Day 61 — spot treatment for acne
+
Benzoyl peroxide 2.5% spot treatment
Apply only on active pimples, at night, after face wash. Minimalist or Acne Star gel. Reduces active acne significantly.
Lifestyle habits that transform skin
① 3L water daily — mandatory for oily, acne-prone skin
② Never touch your face with unwashed hands
③ Change pillowcase every 3–4 days (acne trigger)
④ Wipe face after gym — don't let sweat sit
